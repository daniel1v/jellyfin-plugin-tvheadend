Channels and artwork

DVB EIT itself carries no pictures, though TVHeadend may have artwork from another EPG source. Where a programme or recording has a picture of its own it is used and this setting changes nothing; only where there is none can the channel logo stand in, which at least says which broadcaster the programme came from. A logo used this way is padded into a square rather than stretched to fill the tile.
Jellyfin gives a recording a picture only when it has none, so the first one it is given is the one it keeps. Press this after changing the setting above, or after an upgrade that changed how artwork is published: the pictures are forgotten and fetched again straight away. Live channels and guide entries do not need it; they correct themselves.

Live TV

Each channel being watched is written into a buffer of this size in a circle, so this is the whole disk cost of a channel however long it runs. It is also how far a client may fall behind the live edge, by pausing for instance, before it is moved forward. Around ten minutes of an HD broadcast fit into 512 MB. Changing this needs a server restart.
